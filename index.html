I'm a physicist. Currently I work on making inference faster at mesh.applied (see below for details).

I'm also working on my thesis which is about numerically modelling SiOx (silicon oxide) memristors (a resistance that has memory, meaning its resistance depends on applied current or voltage applied in the past; they can be used as units of non volatile memory or even (if arranged in a crossbar array structure) as matrix-vector multiplicator unit exploiting simple Kirchhoff's and Ohm's law) in the nanojunctions group at Budapest University of Technology.

I also co-founded mesh. in 2023 (builder community and space based in Budapest). Earlier I worked on several projects in high school, including processing of astronomical data (e.g. false coloring Hubble/Webb based images). I also worked at Zeto.eu, where I had to develop testing software and an EEG emulator in C and using a Raspberry 2.
